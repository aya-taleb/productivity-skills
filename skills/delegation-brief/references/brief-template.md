# Brief template

```markdown
# Brief: <task> for <business name>
For: <helper name or role>   From: <user>   Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>

## Outcome
<What done looks like, 1 to 2 lines.>

## Context
<Who it's for and why it matters, up to 3 lines.>

## Steps
1. <one action>
2. <one action>  (careful: <why this step is easy to get wrong>)
...

## Quality bar
Before you say it's done, check:
- [ ] <check>
- [ ] <check>
Example of good: <link or file from the user, or {{example}}>

## Budget and time
- Budget: <user's number or {{budget}}>
- Deadline: <date or {{deadline}}>
- Time limit: <e.g. stop and message me if it passes 3 hours>

## What you can decide
- On your own: <...>
- Message me first: <...>
- Never: <refunds, prices, anything legal, ...>

## Check-ins
- First check: <after the first item / first hour>, by <channel>
- Then: <rhythm>

## Access
- <tool or file>: <how access is shared, e.g. view-only link, separate login>
```
